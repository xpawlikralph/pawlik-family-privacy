Pawlik Family App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Pawlik Family App ("the app") is a private, invite-only app built for use within a single family. It is not a public social network — only people who join a family using that family's private invite code can see that family's data.

What information the app handles

  • Family and member info: names, roles (parent/kid), and a color you choose. Used only to identify who did what within your family's shared data.
  • Chores, calendar events, sticky notes, and rewards: content you and your family members enter, visible only to your family.
  • Documents you upload: photos or PDFs (for example insurance cards or records) you choose to add. Stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket accessible only to your family's members.
  • Location: used only to fetch a local weather forecast for the Overview screen. It is sent to the weather provider for that single request and is not stored by us or associated with your identity.
  • Wi-Fi network name and password: stored only if you choose to add them, so the app can generate a QR code for your family to join your home Wi-Fi. Visible only to your family's members.
  • Camera and photo library access: used only when you choose to attach a photo to a document you're adding.
  • Third-party connections you opt into: if you connect Spotify, we use the resulting access token only to show what's playing and control playback — we do not access your listening history beyond that. If you connect a Philips Hue bridge, the app talks to it directly over your home network; that traffic never passes through our servers.

Third-party services we rely on

  • Supabase — hosts the family database and document storage.
  • Teamup — if your family connects a Teamup calendar, calendar events are read from and written to your own Teamup account under Teamup's own privacy terms.
  • Open-Meteo — provides weather forecasts based on a one-time location lookup.
  • Spotify — only if you choose to connect it, for playback display and control.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, run ads, or use analytics/tracking services. We don't share your family's data with other families or the public. Data you enter is visible only to members of your own family group.

Children's data

A family may include child members, added by a parent. Only the information a parent or the child themselves enters within that private family space is stored, and it is visible only to that family's own members.

Data deletion

You can leave a family at any time from the Family tab. To request deletion of your family's data entirely, contact us using the email below.
